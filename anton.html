Point a laptop at the PA and this listens. It splits the mic into frequency bands, finds the onsets, and locks a tempo by autocorrelating the flux envelope — no click track, no MIDI, nothing to cue. Each of the   songs carries its own palette and its own parameters, so the picture changes with the set, not just with the volume.

The gig setting — listens to the room and reacts to whatever you play.

Your files stay on this machine — nothing is uploaded or published. They're matched to songs by filename. Any song without a file falls back to the demo bed.
